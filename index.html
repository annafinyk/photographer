Hi, I’m Anna

I’m a Ukrainian photographer and content creator based in Hereford. I fell in love with photography the day I bought my first camera, and within a year I was already capturing weddings.

I’m also a TV host of a travel show, so I know how to make people feel relaxed in front of the camera. I’ll guide you through every pose, help with outfits and locations, and catch the real emotions in between.

I photograph weddings, love stories, families, maternity, christenings and individual portraits. I also create photo content for retreats, business camps, events and brands.

Good to know

Booking & terms

Booking

A booking fee secures your date: £50 for photoshoots, 30% for weddings. It is non-refundable.

Rescheduling

You can move your date once if you tell me at least 7 days before. Your booking fee moves with it.

Payment

The balance for photoshoots is paid on the day. For weddings, after the wedding. Bank transfer, invoice on request.

Delivery

Full gallery in 1–2 weeks, in a private online gallery.

Travel

Travel expenses aren’t included in the price and are paid separately for all photoshoots and weddings.
